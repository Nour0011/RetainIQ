AI What-If Retention Simulator

The what-if simulator reuses the trained churn model. It changes one customer attribute, predicts again, and shows how much the churn risk may decrease after the recommended retention action.

How it works

1. Selects the highest-risk customer from the uploaded file.

2. Applies the best recommended retention action.

3. Runs the same trained model again.

4. Compares churn risk before and after the action.
